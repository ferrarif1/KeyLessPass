# Computers & Electrical Engineering submission checklist

Prepared: 11 August 2026

## Requirements basis

- Journal instructions: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-and-electrical-engineering/publish/guide-for-authors>
- Editorial submission system: <https://submit.elsevier.com/COMPELECENG>
- Elsevier's Your Paper Your Way policy permits a flexible initial-submission format, provided that the manuscript is complete, readable, internally consistent, and uses a consistent reference style. Accordingly, the manuscript is supplied in a clean one-column review format rather than being presented as an inferred mandatory Word template.

## Manuscript compliance

- Article type: Research article.
- Abstract: 246 words; concise, self-contained, and without references.
- Keywords: 6.
- Highlights: 5 bullets in a separate editable file; every bullet is at most 85 characters including spaces.
- References: numbered in square brackets and ordered by first appearance; all 21 references are cited and defined.
- Figures: four figures embedded in the manuscript and supplied separately as editable SVG, publication PNG, and PDF artwork.
- Tables: ten editable tables with captions, numbered by first appearance.
- Declarations: CRediT, competing interest, funding, data/code availability, generative-AI use, and acknowledgements are supplied.
- Corresponding author: name, affiliation, postal location, and e-mail are supplied on the title page.
- Supplementary artifact: source, evidence, models, dependency information, and reproduction scripts are packaged separately.
- Graphical abstract: supplied as an optional landscape TIFF at 300 dpi, with matching SVG, PNG, and PDF sources.

## Files prepared for upload

1. `ASTER_CEE_Main_Manuscript_v2.docx` - editable manuscript with author information.

5. `ASTER_CEE_Cover_Letter.docx` - concise editor cover letter.
6. `ASTER_CEE_Declarations.docx` - reusable portal declarations and CRediT statement.
7. `Graphical_Abstract_v2.tif` - optional graphical abstract; SVG, PNG, and PDF variants are also included.
8. `Figure_1_v2.*` through `Figure_4_v2.*` - separate SVG, PNG, and PDF figure files.
9. `ASTER_CEE_Supplementary_Artifact.zip` - reproducibility package.
10. `REVISION_LOG.md` and `DELIVERY_SUMMARY.md` - revision provenance and delivery inventory.

## Portal-only confirmations before pressing Submit

- Confirm the definitive author name and order in the submission portal.
- Add an ORCID only if the author wishes to associate one; none has been inferred.
- Supply a telephone number if the portal marks it as mandatory; none is included in the files because no verified number was available.
- Select the desired subscription/open-access route and license only after reviewing institutional or funder obligations.
- Confirm that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere and that the responsible organization approves submission.
- Enter suggested or opposed reviewers only if requested by the portal; they are intentionally absent from the cover letter.
